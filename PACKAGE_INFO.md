## Description

Maximum-likelihood estimation of bivariate negative binomial (NB2) regression models with Famoye/Sarmanov or discrete-copula (Frank, Gaussian, or Clayton) dependence, and maximum-simulated-likelihood estimation of a bivariate random-parameter negative binomial model under either dependence structure, with a random-coefficient data simulator, diagnostics, and standard model methods.
