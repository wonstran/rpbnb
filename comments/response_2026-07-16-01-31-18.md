### P3 — copula sign coverage

The third-round response's detail sentence claimed both lognormal signs are
tested on the Famoye **and** copula paths, but the committed copula test used
`sign = 1` only. Rather than narrow the wording, I closed the gap: the copula
lognormal prediction test now loops over `sign ∈ {1, −1}`, asserting for each
sign that `predict(fit)` and `predict(fit, newdata = training)` agree and that
`Inf` lands exactly on the `sign · covariate > 0` rows. The statement now holds
as written.

### P3 — `pick_winner()` convergence gating

The helper preferred a converged candidate when exactly one converged, but with
both flags false and finite log-likelihoods it fell through to the likelihood
comparison and returned `"zero"`/`"glmnb"`. It now short-circuits to
`"neither converged"` when neither candidate converged. Verified on the reported
edge case and the three other branches:

```text
both non-converged -> neither converged
zero conv only      -> zero
both conv, glmnb hi -> glmnb
```

The four archived scenarios all have two converged fits, so their reported
winners (`zero`, `zero`, `glmnb`, `zero`) and the response's numerical evidence
are unchanged.
