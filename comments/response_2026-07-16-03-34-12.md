## P2 — fast/slow prediction test tiers

`test-predict-dist.R` performed real RP fits (including two copula fits for the
both-sign lognormal case), so the 24-file set called "fast" was not. Split into
two tiers:

- **Fast, no fitting** — `tests/testthat/test-predict-unit.R` builds a synthetic
  `rpbnb_fit` with stored draws (`helper-slow.R::make_rp_fixture`) and exercises
  the real `predict.rpbnb_fit → .rp_predict_mu → .rp_integrated_mu` path:
  one-row shape, no-`newdata`/`newdata` consistency, lognormal analytic
  infinities for both signs, the `Inf` warning, and estimand metadata — with no
  optimization.
- **Slow, end-to-end** — every fit in `test-predict-dist.R` is now gated behind
  `skip_slow()` (runs only with `RPBNB_RUN_SLOW=1`), keeping the real
  fit-through-predict integration coverage in an explicit slow tier.
